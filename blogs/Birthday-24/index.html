Birthday 24

Well, it's my birthday again! Funny how it comes around every year again.

As per tradition, I went to read my previous birthday post. I have to say that things didn't change that much at all. Well, they did quite a bit, but also it's more of the same?

So, I'll start with the bucket list. I didn't travel Europe at all. I did go to Barcelona for 2 weeks. But that was more to start my new job, meet everyone and enjoy Barcelona. I was supposed to come back and visit again, but those plans fell through. But it's alright. I was okay with it. During my time in Barcelona I did meet a very nice group of Australian people. They adopted me and for my 2 weeks there we just kept going out together to places. It was a really special time for me, and although I didn't talk to them at all since coming back to Singapore, I don't think I will forget them any time soon.

As for the songs, I did learn 2 new classical pieces, but I forgot them, because I didn't practice them for a while. I'm mostly trying to read proper notes now.

I was thinking that I will start this year chronologically. After my last birthday post I was waiting to start my new job. I love it! Everyone there is so nice compared to my previous job and the work itself is really interesting. So in that regard I'm happy. While waiting for this job to start, I traveled Vietnam with a friend from back home. I basically did the same trip I had done when I was traveling Vietnam in 2023. So it was fun! But at times I was a little over it, Vietnam is an okay destination. But I don't think I will go back there anytime soon.

Then I went back to Singapore to start my job and flew to Barcelona. After that I went to Taiwan to hike with Jacey, we hiked HeHuanShan. I didn't post about it, but it was a fun hike. I also did a small getaway with Matan to Thailand. We tried doing a phone detox. We actually managed, and we felt it was fantastic! But it didn't stick at all.

After that I went to ski in Japan again. This was also mostly a goodbye trip to Giat. She was leaving to live in USA with her husband. I hope they are doing well, from what I can see on Instagram they do. For this specific trip, Matan also joined.

In April I went to cycle around Japan. I even posted about it!

And in June I went to hike Yushan! The tallest mountain in Taiwan.

And I think that's pretty much it. For travel-wise, I did start going out with a girl here that I quite like. She's pretty sweet. In hindsight, most of 2026 I felt as if it was mostly around her. She took a pretty big chunk of my headspace. In a good way :)

Naturally, today actually started yesterday for me, as I was cycling across Singapore. God, am I sore. But I'm glad I did that, starting 24 with a bang! And an aching body, which, I heard, starts around this age. So according to schedule. At work everyone greeted me with a happy birthday, and they even got me a cake! And I got quite a lot of messages from people I haven't spoken to in a long time. That was quite nice. And in the evening the girl I'm seeing took me to a very nice steak restaurant, and she absolutely surprised me by getting us both a weekend in Bangkok together. I was completely shocked, but as I said, she's quite sweet :)

And that's it really, I feel like this birthday was actually one of my best. I didn't just come back from a flight from Israel, where I lost twice because I wasn't celebrating it with family and friends, and I was jet-lagged. In hindsight, I'm not sure why I chose those dates.

I felt like this year was a good combination of it being just a regular day, but also a little special. This year did go by quite fast though, I should stop more and smell the flowers, or something like that.

I am going to move to a new house on the 21st of September, so that's quite exciting. I'm going to move with one of my current flat-mates, and the new house looks quite promising. I really can't wait, as I'm a little bit over my current 4 flat-mates situation. And next week I'm going to be hiking Mount Fuji in Japan, so quite a bit to look forward to.

As for the bucket list for next year? Maybe it's time to understand that I'm overshooting, so, for next year I just want to make sure that I keep learning the piano, and also 2-3 more hikes. Doesn't have to be in somewhere specific, just a hike. Oh, and it's definitely time for me to start learning Chinese. I need to sign up for a course finally.
